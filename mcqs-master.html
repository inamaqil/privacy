Privacy Policy

MCQs Master  ·  com.mcqsmaster.app

Last updated: September 20, 2026

✅ The Short Version

  • 👤We use Google Sign-In purely to authenticate you and sync your test progress. We only request your basic profile (email, name, picture).
  • ☁️Your quiz progress, points, and custom test libraries are securely stored in Google Firebase/Firestore so you can access them across devices.
  • 📢Free users see ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect advertising identifiers per their own privacy policy.
  • 💳In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.
  • 🚫We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

1 Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how MCQs Master ("the App") handles information when you use our Android application. The App is developed and maintained by inamaqil (GitHub: @inamaqil).

MCQs Master is an educational platform designed to help you practice multiple-choice questions, create custom test exams, and compete on global leaderboards. We only collect data necessary to provide and improve these core features.

2 Data We Handle

👤

Google Authentication Data

When you log in with Google, we receive your basic profile info (email address, display name, profile picture) to create your account and secure your data.

Google Auth
📊

Test Progress & Leaderboards

Your correct/incorrect answers, subject progress, and total earned points are stored securely in Firestore to build the global leaderboard and sync your learning history.

Firestore Sync
✍️

Custom Test Libraries

If you create and publish custom MCQs exams, this content is stored in our database so it can be shared with the community. You retain ownership of this content.

Firestore Sync
📢

Advertising (Free Users)

Free users see ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect your Advertising ID and usage data to show relevant ads. Pro users receive no ads.

Google AdMob
💳

In-App Purchases (Pro Upgrades)

The Pro upgrade is processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We only receive a confirmation token to unlock your features — no payment data is seen or stored by us.

Google Play

3 Data We Do NOT Collect

The following are never collected by MCQs Master:

  • Your exact physical location or GPS data.
  • Your camera, microphone, or biometric data.
  • Contacts, calendar events, or personal files on your device.
  • Passwords (authentication is handled entirely by Google OAuth).

Policy: play.google.com/about/play-terms

5 Data Retention & Deletion

You have full control over your data. If you wish to delete your account and all associated progress, custom tests, and profile information, you can do so by:

  • Emailing us directly at mcqsmaster.official@gmail.com with your registered Google email address requesting account deletion.

Upon request, your account and all identifiable data will be permanently wiped from our active Firestore database.
